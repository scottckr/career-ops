---
name: No invented product usage claims
description: Never claim applicant uses a company's product personally unless he has explicitly confirmed it
type: feedback
---

Never write cover letter lines or form answers claiming the applicant uses a company's product personally (e.g. "I use Linear every day", "HTTPie is one of my daily tools", "I use Supabase in personal projects") unless the applicant has explicitly confirmed this in the conversation.

**Why:** The applicant has corrected this multiple times — for HTTPie, Grafana, and Linear. These claims are fabrications and feel dishonest. Even when a product _is_ in the applicant's work stack (e.g. Grafana/Elastic at Volvo Cars), that is different from claiming personal daily use.

**How to apply:** Ground "why this company" answers in the JD, the company's public reputation, engineering culture, or the genuine alignment between the role scope and the applicant's experience. Never invent a personal user story.
